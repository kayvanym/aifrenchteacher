# Curriculum — A2 → B1 (12 weeks)

Starting level: A2. Each week has 7 days: days 1–6 bring new content inside the theme,
day 7 is a review day (20-question quiz on the whole week).
Week = ceil(day number / 7). When week 12 is finished, run a level check and write the
next plan (weeks 13–24, towards B1+) here, based on my results and my mistakes.

| Week | Days  | Theme (FR / EN)                                              | Grammar focus                                   | Numbers                              |
|------|-------|--------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 1    | 1–7   | S'installer à Antibes / Moving in and settling down          | passé composé: *avoir* or *être*, agreement     | 100–1 250, prices                    |
| 2    | 8–14  | Les voisins et la copropriété / Neighbours & the copropriété | direct object pronouns (*le, la, les*)          | ordinals (*étage*), dates            |
| 3    | 15–21 | Le marché et les courses / Market & shopping                 | partitive (*du, de la, des*), pronoun *en*      | prices, weights (*500 grammes*)      |
| 4    | 22–28 | Les démarches : banque, poste, mairie / Paperwork            | polite requests: *je voudrais*, *pourriez-vous* | phone numbers, postcodes             |
| 5    | 29–35 | La santé : médecin, pharmacie / Health                       | imparfait (description, habits)                 | times, opening hours                 |
| 6    | 36–42 | Transports : bus, train, aéroport de Nice / Transport        | imparfait vs passé composé                      | timetables (24 h)                    |
| 7    | 43–49 | Loisirs : plage, sport, sorties / Free time                  | comparatives and superlatives                   | temperatures, percentages            |
| 8    | 50–56 | Au restaurant, la cuisine provençale / Food & restaurants    | indirect object pronouns (*lui, leur*)          | bills, 1 000–100 000                 |
| 9    | 57–63 | Le travail et les projets / Work & plans                     | futur simple                                    | years, dates                         |
| 10   | 64–70 | Écrire des messages et des courriels / Writing emails        | conditionnel (politeness, wishes)               | dates in letters, amounts            |
| 11   | 71–77 | Problèmes à la maison : panne, plombier / Problems at home   | pronoun *y*, relative pronouns *qui / que / où* | quantities, measurements             |
| 12   | 78–84 | Donner son avis, l'assemblée générale / Giving opinions      | *il faut que* + subjonctif (introduction)       | mixed review, big numbers            |

## Notes for the teacher
- Recycle words and grammar from earlier weeks in reading texts, conversations and quizzes.
- Keep the content practical for life in Antibes (building, syndic, shops, services).
- Adapt the plan if `mistakes.md` shows a recurring problem: add an extra day on it.
- If I find a week too easy or too hard (see `feedback.md`), adjust the pace and note it in the log.
